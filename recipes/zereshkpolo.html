Zereshk Polo Ba Morgh

Description

Zereshk Polo Ba Morgh is a beautiful and delicious dish of golden, flavourful chicken with rice and tart, sparkling barberries, also called zereshk. It is super fun, easy, and yummy to make!

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Basmati Rice
  • Saffron
  • Spices

Steps

  1. Wash rice
  2. Wash chicken
  3. Cook rice
  4. Strain rice
  5. Cook chicken
  6. Add spices to chicken
  7. Serve and enjoy!
